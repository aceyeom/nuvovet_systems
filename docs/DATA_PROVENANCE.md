## Codebook merge review (2026.10.1)

The audit proposal (`procedure_codes_proposed.json`, 122 codes) was merged with these departures:

- **Dropped as too ambiguous:** bare `발톱` (nail injury is medical), `마취전` (premedication), `응급처치` (not CPR), `유치` (also "indwelling": 유치카테터), `조정` ("용량 조정"), bare `제3안검` (third-eyelid flap is corneal surgery).
- **Prefix-gated instead of general** (count only under that EMR category, via `prefix_synonyms`): `켄넬코프`, `코로나장염`, `ccv`, `인플루엔자`, `civ`, `고양이백혈병` (예방); `전혈`, `혈장` (수액/수혈); `국소` (마취); `백내장`, `단미`, `단이` (수술).
- **Added:** `supersedes` (ICU over general ward, pyometra surgery over spay, cryptorchidectomy over neuter, centesis and phaco over abdominal ultrasound, FNA over skin cytology, SC over IV fluids, after-hours over plain consultation), `coverage_category` on every code (`medical | preventive | non_medical | admin | discount | euthanasia`), `allows_negative_price` on ADM-004, and a few synonyms the held-out strings exposed (`sutureremoval`, `scfluids`, `dentalcleaning`, `lyte`).
- **Diagnoses:** symptom words (`구토`, `설사`, `vomiting`, `diarrhea`) moved from NVD-GI-001 to the symptom-level codes NVD-SYM-002/003; hip dysplasia and Legg-Calvé-Perthes carry the `patella` coverage tag (Korean products group 슬관절·고관절); cruciate disease is its own `cruciate` condition group because whether a knee rider covers it is product-specific.

## Clean-room rebuild plan for drug knowledge

1. **Freeze.** No new features may read compendium-derived fields. The claims engine reads them only for dose references and labels every such finding (`legacy compendium-derived record`).
2. **Sources.** QIA approved labelling (용법·용량, 효능·효과) for Korean vet products, FDA/EMA labels for approved vet drugs, MFDS labels for human drugs used off-label, and primary literature via PubMed.
3. **Process.** Have the LLM extract facts from those sources only, with a citation for every field. A DVM pharmacologist reviews the top 150 drugs by claim frequency first.
4. **Exit criterion.** Delete `backend/data/converted/` once claims-engine dose checks read only the rebuilt store.
